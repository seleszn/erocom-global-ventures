About Erocom

Rooted in the Delta. Trusted far beyond it.

For more than fifteen years, Erocom Global Ventures has been the bridge between the world’s leading feed manufacturers and the farmers who depend on them. What began in 2010 as a livestock feed business in Ekpan-Effurun has grown into a distribution house serving fish farms, poultry and livestock producers, agro-dealers, retailers and investors across the Niger Delta and beyond.

Today, Erocom Global Ventures are major distributors for some of the most respected local and international feed and household brands. We keep genuine stock in three warehouses across Ekpan and Ughelli, close to the farms that rely on it, so no one has to wait on long-haul supply.

Our promise is simple and personal: the right product, genuinely sourced, fairly priced and delivered when you need it.

Our mission

To nourish Nigeria’s farms with genuine, world-class feed and farm inputs, delivered reliably, priced fairly and backed by honest counsel that helps every customer grow.

Our vision

To be the most trusted name in agribusiness distribution across the Niger Delta and beyond, and a lasting partner in building a well-fed, food-secure Nigeria.
